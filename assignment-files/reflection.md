REFLECTION: How do you use ethics and critical thinking in developing front end pages for your application?

We used ethics by being transparent with each other about AI use. We also created and generated unique code without stealing from existing codebases.
For critical thinking, we had to take navigation into account to be able to figure out what makes the most sense for a user to naviagate around our site without getting frustrated. We also had to figure out what functionalities make the most sense where and how to make sure the site is cohesive.
