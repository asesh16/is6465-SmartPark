REFLECTION: How do you use ethics and critical thinking in developing front end pages for your application?

We used ethics by being transparent with each other about AI use. We also created and generated unique code without stealing from existing codebases.
For critical thinking, we had to take navigation into account to be able to figure out what makes the most sense for a user to naviagate around our site without getting frustrated. We also had to figure out what functionalities make the most sense where and how to make sure the site is cohesive.
While developing the Parking Reservation Management portion of SmartPark, we also considered how ethics and critical thinking influence the user's experience. From an ethical perspective, we wanted the reservation process to be transparent and easy to understand. For example, we designed the application to display reservation details, parking costs, and confirmation information so users can review their selections before finalizing a reservation. We also included options to edit and cancel reservations, giving users more control over their bookings.
Critical thinking played an important role in determining how the different pages and functions should work together. We considered the steps a customer would naturally follow when creating, viewing, editing, or canceling a reservation. We also implemented basic validation and checks for conflicting reservations to reduce booking errors. Throughout development, we tested the workflows and adjusted the navigation and page layouts to make the application more consistent and user-friendly. These decisions helped us balance functionality, usability, and responsible application design.
